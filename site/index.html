Tapestry

Lightweight trackpad gestures for macOS.

A small, joyful menu bar app. Tap, hold, or swipe to press a key, run a Shortcut, or open an app.

Get Tapestry Watch the demo

Free. macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.

⌥option

Talk

Tap three fingers to dictate.

Map it to ⌥ Option and your dictation app, like Parrot, starts listening. Your hands stay on the trackpad.

↩return

Send

Tap four to send.

Return, ⌘↩, or any shortcut you like. The prompt goes out without reaching for the keyboard.

Open Claude Terminal

Swipe

Swipe any way.

Up, down, left, right, or on the diagonal. Press any key, run a Shortcut, or open an app or a link.

Learned Four-finger swipe down

Teach

Show it the gesture.

Click Record Gesture, then do it on the trackpad. Tapestry learns the fingers and the direction. Then pick what it does, like ⌃U.

Tap short, still press
Swipe long and on line

Deliberate

Only what you meant.

Taps need a short, still press, so drags never count. Swipes must travel a third of the trackpad and stay on line. Anything in between, and scrolling, stays with macOS.
